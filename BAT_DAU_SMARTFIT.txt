SMARTBUY AI - THU DO ONLINE TU SAN PHAM CO SAN
============================================

Khach hang:
  1. Vao trang san pham tren website SMARTBUY AI.
  2. Bam "Thu do online".
  3. Tai anh ban than hoac chup webcam. Khong can tai anh ao.
  4. Bam "Thu do ngay". Cho PC tao anh va xem ket qua.

CHAY DEMO PC GPU + LAPTOP:
  PC: giai nen thu muc gpu-server-FASHN-8GB, chay INSTALL.bat,
      DOWNLOAD_MODELS.bat, START_PC_LAPTOP.bat (neu da cai model thi bo qua cai).
      PC phai luon bat va online neu dung Tailscale.
  Laptop: copy .env.smartfit.example thanh .env.local, sua IP PC va key.
      Chay START_LAPTOP_WEB.bat. Mo http://localhost:5173.

Kiem tra ket noi truoc: http://localhost:5173/api/smartfit/health
Can CUDA=true va PC GPU da san sang.
